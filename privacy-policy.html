Privacy Policy

Learn how LoanFit collects, uses, and protects your information while you use our platform.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LoanFit.in. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how LoanFit collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit or use our website.

LoanFit is currently under development. As new features become available, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in our services and data handling practices.

2. Information We Collect

At the current stage of development, LoanFit does not require users to create an account or submit loan applications.

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Basic information voluntarily provided through contact forms or email communication.
  • Technical information such as browser type, operating system, device information, and IP address.
  • Anonymous website usage information to help improve performance and user experience.

LoanFit does not intentionally collect sensitive financial information unless such functionality is introduced in future versions of the platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected may be used to:

  • Improve the LoanFit platform and user experience.
  • Respond to your enquiries or support requests.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • Understand website traffic and improve platform performance.

4. Cookies and Analytics

LoanFit may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors interact with our website.

Cookies help us remember user preferences, improve website performance, and analyze website traffic. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

As LoanFit continues to evolve, additional analytics or performance monitoring tools may be introduced to improve our services. Any significant changes to our use of cookies will be reflected in this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Although we strive to use industry-standard security practices, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure. Therefore, LoanFit cannot guarantee absolute security of any information shared online.

As our platform expands and begins handling additional financial information, we will continue strengthening our security practices and infrastructure.

6. Third-Party Services

LoanFit may use trusted third-party services to support website functionality, hosting, analytics, communication, or future financial integrations.

These third-party providers may have their own privacy policies governing how they collect and process information. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any external services they interact with through LoanFit.

LoanFit does not sell or rent users' personal information to third parties.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws and the services offered by LoanFit, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information where legally permitted.
  • Withdraw consent for future communications, where applicable.

As LoanFit introduces additional services and user accounts, we will continue to expand user privacy controls where appropriate.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

LoanFit may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to our platform, new features, legal requirements, or operational changes.

Whenever significant updates are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way LoanFit handles information, please contact us.

Email: info@loanfit.in
Website: www.loanfit.in

We will make every reasonable effort to respond to your enquiry as soon as possible.
